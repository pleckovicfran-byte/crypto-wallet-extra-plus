What's New

VERSION 5.5
Redesigned Navigation

The sidebar is now divided into clear groups, with Add Crypto highlighted as the primary action.

VERSION 5.4
Strict FIFO Pending Transfers

Pending transfers are processed strictly in creation order. Later transfers cannot skip an earlier transfer that is still waiting for funds. Pending balance totals now also show EUR equivalents.

VERSION 5.3
Pending Balance Summary

Shows the total balance required per coin/network, current balance, and remaining shortfall for all pending transfers.

VERSION 5.2
Exchange All

Convert all other cryptocurrency balances into one selected cryptocurrency in a single action.

VERSION 5.1
MAX Amount Buttons

Quickly fill the maximum available balance in Send, Sell and Exchange, in crypto or EUR where supported.

VERSION 5.0
◷ Pending Transactions

Transfers larger than the available balance are saved in the Pending Transactions section.

💶 EUR Balance Removed

Selling crypto deducts it from the portfolio without adding an EUR balance to the app.

⚡ Faster Price Refresh

Market prices refresh automatically about every 10 seconds.

VERSION 4.0
⇄ Crypto-to-Crypto Exchange

You can now exchange any cryptocurrency in your portfolio for another cryptocurrency.

⚡ Automatic Conversion

The app automatically calculates how much of the selected cryptocurrency you will receive using current market prices.

📊 Portfolio Updates

Both cryptocurrency balances are automatically updated after an exchange.

◷ Exchange History

Every exchange is recorded in your transaction history.
